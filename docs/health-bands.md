# Health Bands

Circuit is a credit defense layer, so it should communicate a small number of health bands with clear escalation rules.

## Stable

Buffers are healthy and no action is needed beyond routine monitoring.

## Watch

The position is still serviceable, but a modest adverse move would require intervention planning.

## At risk

The next meaningful move could force action. Alerts should become direct and specific at this stage.

## Critical

The system should assume the window for graceful action is closing and surface the narrowest safe path immediately.
